title: "Does standardization make ridge fair?"
author: "Haoran Sun (hswhhe-uiuc)"
---

Ridge uses standardized predictors so their scales do not determine the penalty.
But if one predictor is mostly measurement noise while another is measured
precisely, does giving them the same penalty still make sense? How could
differences in measurement quality affect our choice of penalty?
