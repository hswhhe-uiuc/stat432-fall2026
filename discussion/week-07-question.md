title: "Does high variance make a pixel useful for QDA?"
author: "Haoran Sun (hswhhe-uiuc)"
---

In Homework 7, we selected pixels with the largest marginal variances
before fitting QDA. Could a pixel have high overall variance but provide
little information about the digit class? Conversely, could a low-variance
pixel still help distinguish classes? How might selecting predictors
using class labels change the analysis, and how should that selection
be handled during cross-validation?
